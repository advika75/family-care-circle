NEUROLOGY CLINIC NOTE (SYNTHETIC)
Patient: 31 y/o female with migraine without aura.
Subjective: 6-8 migraine days per month with throbbing headache, nausea and light sensitivity.
Assessment: Episodic migraine, increasing frequency; candidate for preventive therapy.
Plan:
1. Start propranolol 40 mg PO BID for migraine prevention.
2. Sumatriptan 50 mg PO at onset of migraine; may repeat once after 2 hours. Max 200 mg per day.
3. Naproxen 500 mg PO BID PRN headache, no more than 10 days per month.
4. Keep a headache diary for 8 weeks.
Avoid skipping meals; regular sleep schedule. Follow up in 8 weeks.
