CARDIOLOGY CLINIC NOTE (SYNTHETIC)
Patient: 71 y/o female with new atrial fibrillation.
Subjective: Palpitations and exertional fatigue for 3 weeks. Denies syncope.
Objective: HR 112, irregularly irregular. Echo: preserved EF.
Assessment: Atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response; CHA2DS2-VASc 4.
Plan:
1. Start apixaban 5 mg PO BID for stroke prevention. Do not skip doses.
2. Start metoprolol succinate 50 mg PO once daily for rate control.
3. Avoid NSAIDs such as ibuprofen while on apixaban; use acetaminophen 500 mg q6h PRN for pain.
Report any unusual bleeding, black stools, or dizziness. Holter monitor for 48 hours. Follow up in 4 weeks.
