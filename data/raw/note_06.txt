TELEHEALTH VISIT NOTE (SYNTHETIC)
Patient: 27 y/o female, not pregnant.
Subjective: 2 days of dysuria, urinary frequency and urgency, and suprapubic pain. No fever, no flank pain, no vaginal discharge.
Assessment: Acute uncomplicated cystitis (urinary tract infection).
Plan:
1. Nitrofurantoin 100 mg PO BID x 5 days. Take with food.
2. Phenazopyridine 200 mg PO TID x 2 days for burning with urination; may turn urine orange.
Increase water intake. Return or seek care for fever, flank pain, vomiting, or symptoms not improving after 48 hours.
