## Results: MTSamples (100 real notes) vs. synthetic (labeled separately — different datasets, not directly comparable)

|---|---|---|
| Source FKGL | 9.82 ± 1.96 | 8.05 ± 1.19 |
| Patient FKGL (Δ vs source) | 5.31 ± 1.00 (−4.51) | 5.36 ± 0.79 (−2.69) |
| Caregiver FKGL (Δ) | 8.05 ± 3.58 (−1.78) | 6.65 ± 1.28 (n/a in Table 1) |
| Child FKGL (Δ) | 2.77 ± 0.97 (−7.05) | 3.16 ± 1.03 (−4.89) |
| Patient ROUGE-1 / ROUGE-L | 0.254 / 0.139 | 0.29 / 0.21 |
| Caregiver ROUGE-1 / ROUGE-L | 0.198 / 0.107 | 0.46 / 0.31 |
| Child ROUGE-1 / ROUGE-L | 0.098 / 0.065 | 0.19 / 0.13 |

Real MTSamples notes are longer and more complex (higher source FKGL, more varied content: imaging
measurements, lab values, surgical detail), so ROUGE against the source drops for every persona — expected,
since there's proportionally less of the (longer) note being copied forward.

### Entity retention (medication/dosage/frequency/duration)

| | MTSamples patient | MTSamples caregiver | Synthetic patient (seed0, 12 notes) | Synthetic caregiver (seed0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st attempt (model only) | 74.1% ± 33.5 | **43.5% ± 40.3** | 81.0% ± 13.3 | 85.4% ± 13.9 |
| After retries, before fallback | 94.8% ± 18.4 | 95.6% ± 16.5 | 96.3% ± 4.5 | 100.0% ± 0.0 |
| Final (with fallback) | 100.0% | 100.0% | 100.0% | 100.0% |

The caregiver checklist's first-attempt retention on real notes (43.5%) is far below synthetic (85.4%) — the
model misses roughly twice as many required facts on its first try against real clinical notes before the
retry loop and fallback recover them. The safety net (retry + deterministic fallback) does its job: final
retention is 100% in both datasets.

### Hallucination proxy (unsupported numbers/medications)

| | MTSamples | Synthetic baseline (seed0, 12 notes) |
|---|---|---|
| Patient: notes with unsupported numbers | 26/100 (mean 0.39/note) | 1/12 (mean 0.08/note) |
| Caregiver: notes with unsupported numbers | **73/100** (mean 1.56/note) | 2/12 (mean 0.17/note) |
| Child: notes with unsupported numbers | 1/100 | 0/12 |
| Notes with any unsupported medication (any persona) | 9/100 | 0/12 |

This is the largest dataset-driven gap. Real notes carry far more numeric detail (vitals, lab values, imaging
measurements) that the checklist can misstate or invent around, and the checker (per the README's documented
limitation) flags any number/unit pairing it can't match verbatim or via its normalization table — so some
flagged items may be paraphrases rather than true fabrications. The medication list, however, names specific
drugs never mentioned in the source, which is a cleaner signal (see examples below).

### Child reading level vs. the ~1st-grade target

Mean child FKGL on MTSamples: 2.77 ± 0.97 (median 2.75). Only **4/100** notes are at or below true 1st-grade
(FKGL ≤ 1.0); 63/100 are at or below the project's own enforced ceiling of grade 3; 37/100 exceed it. This
matches the synthetic result (child FKGL ~2.9–3.2 even with the strict v2 prompt) — on both datasets, the
"strict" child prompt lands around 3rd-grade reading level, not literal 1st grade.

### NER vs. gold

**Unavailable.** 0 of 30 selected notes have a completed manual annotation (manifest status `pending` for all
30); the stub gold files have `entities: []`. No NER precision/recall/F1 is reported for MTSamples. (Synthetic
NER, unaffected: micro F1 0.96 exact / 0.98 relaxed over 6 hand-annotated notes — unchanged, not re-run.)

**Fallback examples:** `mtsamples_Acute_Inferior_Myocardial_Infarction` (caregiver omitted 50 mg after 2

**Hallucination examples (unsupported_content):**
- `mtsamples_2-D_Echocardiogram_-_1` (caregiver): invents "follow-up echocardiogram in 6 months" — not in source.
- `mtsamples_AV_Fistula_-_1` (caregiver): invents "check vitals every hour for 6 hours" — not in source.
- `mtsamples_Accidental_Celesta_Ingestion_-_ER_Visit` (caregiver): source says a 3-year-old ingested two to

  dosage instruction in a pediatric poisoning case**, the most safety-relevant finding in this run.
- `mtsamples_Total_Knee_Replacement`, `mtsamples_Umbilical_Hernia_Repair` (patient_summary): invent specific
  OTC drug names ("naproxen", "ibuprofen", "tylenol") never mentioned in the source.

  (caregiver): all three independently invent "aspirin" as a post-procedure instruction — a plausible-sounding,
  pattern-matched recommendation not grounded in any of these three source notes.
- Other invented medications: morphine (`mtsamples_Laminotomy_Facetectomy_Foraminotomy`), tramadol
  (`mtsamples_Shunt_Revision`) — both opioids, not mentioned in their source notes.

**Child reading level examples:** `mtsamples_Blepharoplasty_-_Direct_Brow_Repair` (grade 3.3), `mtsamples_Accidental_Celesta_Ingestion_-_ER_Visit` (3.2), `mtsamples_Aortobifemoral_Bypass_-_1` (3.1).

**Repetition examples:** `mtsamples_Accidental_Celesta_Ingestion_-_ER_Visit`, `mtsamples_Attempted_Suicide_-_Consult`, `mtsamples_Bunionectomy_Osteotomy` (all caregiver, one bullet repeated verbatim later in the list).

## Fixes (status update)

Of the 3 proposed fixes, **1 and 2 were approved and are now implemented** (commits `ecf010f` and `237977e`);
**fix 3 (truncation/repetition) was not approved** and remains an unimplemented proposal below.

1. **IMPLEMENTED — caregiver/patient medication hallucination guardrail.** `generate_with_retries` now also
   runs the existing `unsupported_meds` check on every attempt and retries with feedback naming the invented
   drug (`src/postprocess.py`, `src/prompts.py::build_retry_messages`).
   **Measured before/after** (11 notes re-run with real `llama_cpp` inference, outputs in
   `data/public/mtsamples/outputs_postfix/` vs. the original `outputs/`): hallucinated medication mentions
   across these notes fell from **12 to 1** (e.g. `mtsamples_Aortobifemoral_Bypass_-_1`,
   `mtsamples_Arterial_Imaging`, `mtsamples_Carotid_Cerebral_Arteriograms`: invented "aspirin" → gone;
   `mtsamples_Shunt_Revision`: "tramadol" → gone; `mtsamples_Laminotomy_Facetectomy_Foraminotomy`: "morphine" →
   gone; `mtsamples_Total_Knee_Replacement`, `mtsamples_Umbilical_Hernia_Repair`, `mtsamples_Epididymectomy`:
   invented OTC names → gone). One residual case: `mtsamples_Escharotomy`'s patient summary still names
   "acetaminophen" after 2 retries (down from 2 invented drugs to 1) — the fix reduces but, by design (best
   attempt kept after `max_retries`), does not guarantee zero.

2. **IMPLEMENTED — dosage-directive hard validation.** `redact_contradicted_dosages` (`src/postprocess.py`)
   checks directive dosing language ("give/administer/take ... <dose>") against the normalized source text and
   replaces a dose that appears nowhere in the source with a safe placeholder, forcing `fallback_used`.
   **Important correction during implementation:** the first version checked against NER-extracted DOSAGE
   entities instead of the raw source text, which produced false positives — the rule-based NER misses
   spelled-out doses ("81 milligrams", "One mL") that are genuinely in the source
   (`mtsamples_Acute_Inferior_Myocardial_Infarction`, `mtsamples_Carpal_Ligament_Release_-_2`), and the
   entity-only check would have redacted correct information. Fixed before measuring.
   **Measured before/after** (same 11-note re-run): contradicted dosage directives fell from **6 to 0**,
   including the clinically dangerous case: `mtsamples_Accidental_Celesta_Ingestion_-_ER_Visit`'s caregiver
   checklist now reads "Give the patient 40 mg of Celesta" (matching the source) instead of the original
   "give the patient 20 mg" — in this run the ordinary retry loop self-corrected it before redaction was even
   needed, with the hard-validation redact as the backstop for cases where a retry doesn't self-correct (as
   seen in the other 5 notes, e.g. `mtsamples_Weight_Gain_and_Edema`'s invented "10 mg" lisinopril dose, which
   the source never states at all).

   | | Hallucinated meds (11 notes, all personas) | Contradicted dosage directives (11 notes) |
   |---|---|---|
   | Before | 12 | 6 |
   | After | 1 | 0 |

3. **NOT APPROVED / not implemented — truncation/repetition compounding on long real notes.**
   `mtsamples_Multiple_Medical_Problems` degenerates into a repeated line before hitting `max_new_tokens=512`;
   4/100 notes truncate outright (`mtsamples_Infantile_Spasms`,
   `mtsamples_Multiple_Medical_Problems_-_Discharge_Summary`, +2 more). Proposal unchanged: raise
   `personas.patient_summary.max_new_tokens` for the `mtsamples` dataset and add repetition-triggered early
   stopping in `src/generator.py`.

## Open issues / caveats

- Inference for the 100-note run predates this session; I verified but did not regenerate it (see "Honesty
  note" above). If reproducibility from a clean state matters, re-running with `--force` is one command away,
  but will take roughly 1–1.5 hours on this hardware (mean 62.8 s/note × 100, observed range 21.6–206.3 s).
- NER quality on MTSamples is simply unknown — 0 of 30 annotations are complete. Completing even a handful would
  let `evaluate_mtsamples_ner.py` report real numbers instead of "unavailable."
- The "unsupported numbers" hallucination proxy is noisy on real notes (see caveat above); treat the *count* as
  an upper bound on true hallucinations and the specific drug-name examples as the more reliable signal.
- Three stray duplicate result files exist at the repo root (see "Data integrity notes"); not deleted pending
  your confirmation.
